Exam can't start in this mode

You're in a private (Incognito) window. CheatNoNo can't protect the exam here.

  1. Easiest: close this window, open a normal Chrome window, and open your exam link again.
  2. Or open Chrome menu → Extensions → Manage extensions → CheatNoNo → Details and turn on Allow in Incognito.
  3. Come back to this page and select Check again.
