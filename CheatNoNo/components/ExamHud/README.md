The exam HUD is the always-visible badge, top-right of the exam page, that shows protection and strike count, for example Protected, Strikes 1/3.

Variants: calm (0 strikes, `brand-strong` icon), warning (`cn-hud--warn`), final (`cn-hud--danger`), record-only (`cn-hud--record`, reads Recording) and collapsed (`cn-hud--mini`, icon only, under 480px wide). The consumer provides the strike count and mode.

Do keep it small and out of the way of the questions. Don't remove the printed count in the collapsed state's accessible label.
