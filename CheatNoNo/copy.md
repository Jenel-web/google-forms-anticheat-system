# Copy

The words used in the screens. Keep this voice: plain, calm, specific, never accusing.

## Student overlays

| Screen | Text |
| --- | --- |
| Consent title | Before you begin |
| Monitored | Leaving the exam tab, and for how long. Websites you open (domain only, never page content). Leaving fullscreen. Copy and paste. |
| Blocked | AI assistants such as ChatGPT and Gemini. Search engines and translation tools. Messaging and file-sharing sites. |
| Consent checkbox | I understand what is monitored and blocked, and I agree to take this exam under these rules. |
| Start | Start exam. Helper: Starting the exam opens fullscreen. |
| Not signed in | You're not signed in to Google. Sign in with the account your teacher expects, then reload this page. |
| Incognito | Exam can't start in this mode. You're in a private (Incognito) window. CheatNoNo can't protect the exam here. |
| Unsupported browser | This browser isn't supported. CheatNoNo exams run in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop computer. |
| HUD | Protected, Strikes 1/3. Record-only: Recording, Strikes 0/3. |
| Strike 1 | Strike 1 of 3. You left the exam tab. This is a friendly warning. A third strike pauses your exam until your teacher unlocks it. Button: Continue exam. |
| Strike 2 | Strike 2 of 3, Final warning. One more and your exam pauses. Your questions are hidden until you return. Your answers so far are saved. Button: Return to exam. |
| Locked | Your exam is paused. Waiting for your teacher to unlock it. Message your teacher (optional). Your answers are saved. Keep this tab open. |
| Fullscreen | Return to fullscreen to continue. The exam is hidden until you are back in fullscreen. |
| Blocked site | chatgpt.com. Blocked during your exam. This attempt was recorded. Strike 1 of 3 added. Button: Back to exam. Record-only: No strike was added. Your teacher can see this attempt. |
| Popup idle | Not on an exam. Open the exam link from your teacher and CheatNoNo starts by itself. |
